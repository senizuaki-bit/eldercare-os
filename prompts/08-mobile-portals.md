# M08 — Elder, caregiver and family mobile PWA refinement

```text
Execute M08 only.

Goal:
Turn the mobile shell into three usable, accessible role-specific experiences.

Deliver:
1. PWA manifest, installability, responsive shell and offline/error handling.
2. Elder home: dominant voice request, emergency, today schedule/reminders, contact human/family, recent service and rating.
3. Caregiver home: urgent task, my tasks, nearby/same-floor work, shift and handover.
4. Family home: elder switcher, daily summary, events requiring attention, service progress, trends and communication drafts.
5. Role-specific notification center.
6. Large touch targets, semantic labels, visible focus, high contrast and reduced motion.
7. Network interruption states and retry; do not show false success.
8. Privacy explanations and consent controls.
9. Playwright mobile E2E at 360x800 and 375x812.
10. Accessibility automated scan and documented manual checks.

Visual direction:
- Use the references as inspiration for friendly card composition and bottom navigation.
- Do not copy their property-management wording, brands or exact icon grid.
- Elder mode must be substantially simpler and larger than ordinary consumer apps.

Acceptance:
- Each role can complete its core task with minimal steps.
- Family cannot discover caregiver live location or raw transcript through UI/API.
```
